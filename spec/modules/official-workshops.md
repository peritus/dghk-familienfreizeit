# Official workshops

Module id `official-workshops` · factory `officialWorkshops`

Official workshops supplies the workshop-specific facts for organiser-authored
official sessions and for sessions later supplied by the deferred
[call for papers](../deferred-ideas/call-for-papers.md) contract. It does not own
proposal review, ranking, scheduling, or attendance allocation.

## 1. Dependencies and ownership

Requires [programme core](programme-core.md). The call-for-papers contract is an
optional later source for official sessions.
It owns official workshop content, audience and age eligibility, capacity,
leaders, cancellation, and the official scheduleable-session contribution.

An organiser-authored session becomes eligible for ranking when it is published
into an active candidate round. A call-for-papers source may create the same kind
of round after review and selection. Eligibility is evaluated per person for
candidate visibility and again by attendance allocation.

## 2. Schedule boundary

An official session has no required slot or space before scheduling. The module
supplies its content and constraints to [workshop scheduling](workshop-scheduling.md),
which may produce one or more offerings for the same session.

## 3. Tests

Fixtures cover official content, audience and age filtering, leaders, capacity,
cancellation, selected sessions without a placement, and multiple offerings for
one session.
